# Case F — Thin balanced depth and display rounding

**Source:** R&D §2.5; “the same day” following the November 6, 2026 examples.
Original capture time is unknown. Historical PROD Core Exchange Conservative
workflow on Novig; no blended chain-link icon was shown.

## Opportunity, display and venue prices

Arkansas State **+25.5 against Alabama** displayed **3.08% advertised EV**.

| Evidence scope | Intended +25.5 side | Opposing −25.5 side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OddsJam listing | Rounded 51.0¢, $302 liquidity | Not specified in the listing narrative |
| Best venue quote | 50.5¢, $281 | 50.0¢, $250 |
| Visible venue levels | Approximately $357 across 50.5–52.0¢ | Approximately $380 across 50.0–51.0¢ |

Both sides were thin, with nearly balanced aggregate depth. The owner placed more
weight on advertised EV while recognizing sparse markets can make price signals
less reliable. Compared with C, opposing depth gave less directional encouragement.

## Decision and recorded execution

The owner rated the setup **approximately 5–6/10**, a marginal execute/pass choice,
and ultimately executed **+25.5 at 50.5¢ inside Novig**. Bet Tracker recorded
**−102 American odds**, an approximate odds representation of that venue price.
OddsJam's rounded **51.0¢ was not the execution price**. The narrative does not
supply a final executed stake; do not infer one from displayed liquidity.

The source attributes the price difference to display rounding. The reason for
**$302 versus $281 liquidity** remains unconfirmed: quote movement, consumption or
a reading/transcription difference are possible explanations, not findings. Absence
of the chain-link icon means the listing was not identified as a blended calculation;
do not label the discrepancy a blend or automatically call it execution slippage.

The **Liq <= $500** tag describes liquidity at the selected single execution price,
not all levels or the opposite-side book.
