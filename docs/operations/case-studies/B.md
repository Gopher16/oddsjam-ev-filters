# Case B — Lower advertised EV, greater opposing depth

**Source:** R&D §2.1 and rating in §2.5; November 7, 2026 opportunity. Screenshot
capture time is unknown. Historical PROD Core Exchange Conservative workflow on Novig.

## Opportunity and depth

Samford vs. UNLV, **Over 158.5**, displayed **3.07% advertised EV**.

| Scope | Intended Over side | Opposing Under side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best displayed level | $310 at 47.5¢ (approximately +111 American) | $2,048 at 53.0¢ |
| Broader visible levels | $716 across 47.5–49.0¢ | $9,173 across 53.0–54.0¢ |

The best-level amounts and broader totals describe different depth scopes; do not
add them as independent liquidity pools or assume all levels can be taken at 47.5¢.

## Reasoning, decision and tracking

The owner considered this distribution more favorable for taking the Over than
A's large same-side concentration and reported placing a **$140 funded wager**, using
the quarter-Kelly recommendation against the dated $20,000 sizing setting. The
illustrative assessment is **9/10**, not a probability estimate.

The Bet Tracker tag **Liq <= $500** represents the **$310 at the selected price**,
not $716 of broader same-side depth or $9,173 on the opposite side. The reported
stake is distinct from displayed liquidity; this summary does not independently
verify venue fills, net fees or settlement.
