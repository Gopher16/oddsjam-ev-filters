## Opportunity and missing context

Massachusetts **+6 against Boston College** displayed **2.53% advertised EV**,
**−105 American odds**, **$926 posted liquidity** and a **$130 Kelly recommendation**.
BetOpenly did not provide a visible order book in this workflow, so opposing-side
liquidity and deeper levels could not be assessed. This is missing evidence, not
zero depth, an observation of balance or permission to invent book snapshots.

## Reasoning, decision and tracking

The owner gave the setup **7/10** and reported executing the wager. Their favorable
historical impression of BetOpenly encouraged taking qualifying advertised-positive-EV
opportunities with less additional order-book scrutiny. Substantially greater
same-side liquidity would have prompted more review, but no numerical veto threshold
is specified.

The **$500 < Liq <= $1K** tag reflects the **$926 at the selected price**. The source
reports the $130 recommendation and an executed decision, but does not separately
state a confirmed final filled stake in this narrative. Keep that recommendation
separate from an independently verified execution amount or fee-net payout.
