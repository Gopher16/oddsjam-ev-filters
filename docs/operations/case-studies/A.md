# Case A — High advertised EV, discretionary pass

**Source:** R&D §2.1 and illustrative rating in §2.5; November 7, 2026 opportunity.
Original screenshot capture time is unknown. Historical PROD Core Exchange
Conservative workflow on Novig; this precedes the strategy's TEST-only transition.

## Opportunity and visible quote

Western Carolina vs. UNC Asheville, **Under 151.5**, displayed **13.23% advertised EV**.
The source reports $7,446 on the intended Under side at 46.5¢ (approximately +115
American), versus roughly $100 on the opposing Over side at 54.0¢. These are posted
quote descriptions, not proof of executable size or informed order ownership.

## Reasoning, decision and tracking

The owner passed despite the unusually large advertised EV: the same-side concentration
raised concern about taking a quote against potentially informed resting orders.
The later illustrative assessment is **3/10**. No wager fill or stake is reported for
this passed opportunity. Do not turn its posted liquidity into an executed amount.

A PROD pass remains unlogged in Bet Tracker under the owner's convention, preserving
later reconsideration without a Previously Placed checkmark. There is no evidence
that the proposed TEST-only pass-tag practice was used here.
