# Programme requirement coverage

REQ IDs identify stable capability families from the owner roadmaps, not additional
PRs or new acceptance policy. Section locators refer to the frozen R&D source; the
PR roadmap cards remain the detailed scope/acceptance baseline. This is a concise
public mapping, not publication of the private Word documents. Source authority and
fingerprints are in [source baselines](SOURCE_BASELINES.md).

Every capability below has a baseline roadmap destination. Merged documentation does
not mean downstream software, data or research evidence is delivered. R34–R60 are
conditional explorations; preserve their feasibility, source-access and activation
gates. No family is retired by this mapping. As later assessment refines a family,
append subrequirements without renumbering it and preserve all original intent.

## Cross-cutting acceptance throughout the programme

All families retain source hashes, known/unknown timing, funding/product/regime
separation, exact metric denominators and immutable histories. Discovery breadth
never forces unsupported winners. Confidence/credible/predictive intervals retain
their distinct meanings; simulations do not prove edge. Manual rules have triggers,
evidence, missing-data behavior and versioned decisions. New venues/DFS products
need their own economic/rules contracts. Refitting/scenario controls never silently
change an approved study. Account access, paid feeds and live trading need separate
authorization. These obligations apply to each linked PR, not only R03 or R60.

The integrated journey is refresh → scope/brief → a small supported TEST queue →
versioned study monitoring → linked EV/exposure/model investigation → reproducible
risk scenario → recorded continue/modify/pause/stop decision. Sections 5.2–5.7 are
connected requirements, not instructions to add six disconnected dashboard pages.
PrimeDope is the functional reference for REQ18–REQ21; sports-betting economics,
correlation, uncertainty and ruin definitions must be independently validated.

Long-term capture is bounded by monitored feeds/periods and cannot repair unseen
historical decisions. Player simulations may feed betting and fantasy, while
contest scoring, field/ownership, season/draft and execution contracts remain
separate. Sidekick is a product-experience reference, not a validated implementation
or permission to copy proprietary models.

At phase boundaries reconcile every family and the detailed selected PR cards:
delivered with evidence, planned, moved with destination, deferred with reason, or
retired only with explicit owner agreement. A single broad REQ row does not waive
any detailed baseline acceptance clause. See [delivery workflow](DELIVERY_WORKFLOW.md).
