## Problem and outcome

A contributor can currently learn the research dashboard but cannot readily trace
how a surfaced OddsJam quote becomes an accepted execution, discretionary pass,
paper record or manual CSV input. R01 makes that operating context discoverable,
with a dated settings matrix and clear evidence boundaries. A reader should be
able to explain the November 10 transition and why logged paper stakes and displayed
liquidity are not verified fills without consulting private Word files.
